# How to Prove Your AI Agent Is Actually Worth It

## Overview: The Core Thesis
In this talk, Inês Bolaños (Senior Product Analyst at PagerDuty) addresses the massive gap between building AI agents and actually proving their business value. She argues that the industry is suffering from "Agent Washing"—rebranding standard automation as AI just to ride the hype wave. 

To combat this, she introduces the **HIRE Framework** to determine if an AI agent should even be built, and presents a new set of **Agentic Product Metrics** and an open-source evaluation pipeline designed to shift analytics from deterministic button clicks to probabilistic, conversational success.

## Key Concepts & Frameworks
